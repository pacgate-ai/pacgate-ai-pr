DELETE-IMPACT VERDICT: can C:\pacgate-ai-pr be deleted safely?
=============================================================

--- Named volumes: where do they physically live? ---
  pacgate-ai-bundle_pacgate-db-data
      mountpoint: /var/lib/docker/volumes/pacgate-ai-bundle_pacgate-db-data/_data
  qm-pacgate-pgdata
      mountpoint: /var/lib/docker/volumes/qm-pacgate-pgdata/_data
  qm-pacgate-coredata
      mountpoint: /var/lib/docker/volumes/qm-pacgate-coredata/_data

  => Named volumes live inside the Docker VM, NOT under C:\pacgate-ai-pr.
     So the DATABASES themselves survive a directory deletion.

--- But the CONTAINERS would lose their data anyway, because: ---
  1. deer-flow bind-mounts ./data -> C:\pacgate-ai-pr\deploy\client-bundle\data
     Its live SQLite (deerflow.db, checkpoints.db) lives THERE, not in a volume.
     1,635 MB of conversation checkpoints would be gone.
  2. pacgate-api bind-mounts ./data -> same directory.
  3. BOTH compose projects record config paths under pacgate-ai-pr, so
     `docker compose down/up/config` from the repo would FAIL to find its files.

--- containers affected: 14 ---
  - deer-flow
  - deer-flow-frontend
  - openviking
  - pacgate-api
  - pacgate-db
  - pacgate-mcp
  - pacgate-nginx
  - qm-pacgate-admin-1
  - qm-pacgate-auth-1
  - qm-pacgate-core-1
  - qm-pacgate-mailpit-1

  - qm-pacgate-portal-1
  - qm-pacgate-web-ui-1

--- FINAL VERDICT ---

  NO. Deleting C:\pacgate-ai-pr would NOT leave everything running perfectly.

  It would:
    - dangle 19 bind mounts across 6 containers
    - destroy 1,635 MB of live deer-flow checkpoints (untracked, only copy)
    - lose the live deerflow.db (admin credentials, threads, users)
    - break `docker compose` for 2 projects (recorded config paths)

  It would NOT destroy:
    - the named Postgres volumes (they live in the Docker VM)
    - the 23.8 MB tracked source (recoverable from GitHub remotes)

  => The correct order is MIGRATE, then delete. Never delete first.
